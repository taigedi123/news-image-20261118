# The World in One Day — Wednesday 18 November 2026

One image summing up the world news of Wednesday 18 November 2026,
drawn in code with Pillow (`draw.py` → `entry.png`, 1024×1024).

Five stories: the Xi–Putin–Trump summit gathering in Shenzhen; Ukraine
striking the Kerch Bridge for a second day, collapsing a span; US jets
striking an Iranian missile site on Qeshm Island and Iran firing drones
at Bahrain and Kuwait; Democrat Mary Peltola winning Alaska's Senate
seat; and the Ebola outbreak in DR Congo passing 11,000 cases. Also
noted: Voyager 1 reaching one light-day from Earth, the US losing its
measles elimination status, and Cyclone Xavier hitting the Cook Islands.

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` to the output directory, as the kit requires.
